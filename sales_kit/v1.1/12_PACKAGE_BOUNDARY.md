# Package Boundary

This Seller Sales Kit is internal-only and must remain separate from the Buyer Delivery ZIP.

Buyer receives:
- CK-NEXUS-Creator-Source-Sale-Package-v1.zip
- its SHA-256 sidecar
- executed quotation/commercial schedule as applicable

Seller retains:
- internal price floor/negotiation guidance
- lead qualification notes
- closing scripts
- claims guardrails

Do not merge seller credentials, personal accounts, private data or internal negotiation notes into any buyer delivery artifact.
