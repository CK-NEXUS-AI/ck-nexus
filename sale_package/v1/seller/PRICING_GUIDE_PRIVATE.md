# Seller-Only Pricing Guide — Do Not Include in Buyer ZIP

Working negotiation guide for the current Creator Source/Beta condition:

- Fast-close range: THB 59,000–79,000.
- Practical target range: THB 99,000–129,000.
- Suggested asking price: THB 149,000.
- Source transfer with additional handover/custom installation/support can justify a higher negotiated total when the scope is written clearly.

Do not price Production Ready/Enterprise certification into the current package unless the corresponding Q10-Q16 work is actually completed and evidenced.

Adjust final price for exclusivity/IP assignment, support duration, customization, tax, payment risk and whether the seller keeps any reuse/resale rights.
