## Important correction

The phrases such as **"66 free credits daily"**, **"unlimited free generations"**, **"100% free"**, and **"30 videos/month"** are treated as **USER_CLAIM**, not as verified facts. Free web access, free credits, free API usage, and commercial-use permission are separate properties.

## Audit rule

Do not purchase a subscription solely because a free tier is exhausted. First check: current quota, reset period, watermark, resolution, queue, commercial-use terms, API cost, model availability, and whether the free tier is geographically/account restricted.
